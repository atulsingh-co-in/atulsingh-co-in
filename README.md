### 🧭 How I work

1. **Find the gap.** Where does the team lose time or miss opportunities today?
2. **Find the fit.** Where does AI genuinely help, and where doesn't it?
3. **Build and train.** Build the workflow, deploy it, and train the team until they own it.

Choosing tools comes last. In my experience the biggest blocker is habit and hesitation, especially in experienced people, which is why training is part of every build.
